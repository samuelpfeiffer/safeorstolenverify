## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Inconsistent NAP** - Different address on website vs. Google  
❌ **Incomplete Profile** - Missing photos, hours, services  
❌ **No Posts** - Google favors active businesses  
❌ **Ignoring Reviews** - Not responding hurts rankings  
❌ **Wrong Category** - Miscategorizing confuses Google  
❌ **Keyword Stuffing** - Don't spam keywords in description  
❌ **Fake Reviews** - Google detects and penalizes this  
❌ **Multiple Profiles** - Creates duplicate business confusion
