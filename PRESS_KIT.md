## 📝 Company Overview

### Short Description (100 words)

SafeOrStolen is a consumer protection platform that helps people verify whether second-hand items are stolen before making a purchase. By checking IMEI numbers, VIN numbers, and serial numbers against global stolen item databases, we protect buyers from financial loss, legal troubles, and inadvertently supporting criminal activity. Our free verification service is available via web, iOS, and Android applications, making it accessible to anyone, anywhere.

### Long Description (250 words)

SafeOrStolen addresses a critical problem in the second-hand marketplace: the risk of purchasing stolen goods. With online marketplaces and peer-to-peer sales growing exponentially, consumers face increasing exposure to stolen merchandise. Buying stolen property can result in:

- Financial loss ($500-$5000 average per incident)
- Confiscation of purchased items by law enforcement
- Legal charges for possession of stolen property
- Inadvertently funding organized crime

Our platform provides instant, free verification for items using their unique identifiers (IMEI for phones, VIN for vehicles, serial numbers for electronics and tools). Users simply enter the item's identification number and receive immediate results from our comprehensive database of reported stolen items.

**Key Features:**
- Free instant verification
- Support for IMEI, VIN, and serial numbers
- Global stolen item database access
- Mobile and web platforms
- Privacy-focused verification
- Real-time results

**Impact to Date:**
- 500,000+ items verified
- Featured on Times Square billboard
- Available in 190+ countries
- Preventing fraud and protecting consumers daily
- Supporting law enforcement recovery efforts

SafeOrStolen empowers consumers to make informed purchasing decisions while disrupting the market for stolen goods. By making verification free and accessible, we're creating a safer marketplace for everyone.

## 💡 Our Solution

### How It Works

**3-Step Verification Process:**

1. **Enter Information**
   - User provides item's unique identifier
   - Accepts IMEI, VIN, or serial number
   - Simple, intuitive interface

2. **Instant Check**
   - System queries global stolen item databases
   - Checks against police reports and registries
   - Real-time processing (< 2 seconds)

3. **Clear Results**
   - Safe: Item has no stolen reports
   - Warning: Item flagged as stolen or suspicious
   - Detailed explanation with next steps
