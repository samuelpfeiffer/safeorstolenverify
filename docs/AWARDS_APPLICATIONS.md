### Materials to Prepare:
- [ ] Company one-pager (PDF)
- [ ] Executive summary (250 words)
- [ ] Key metrics and statistics
- [ ] Customer testimonials
- [ ] Product screenshots (high-res)
- [ ] Demo video (60 seconds)
- [ ] Founder bio and headshot
- [ ] Press coverage compilation
- [ ] Technology differentiation summary

3. **3-Second Verification**: Fastest in the industry

## 💡 Tips for Winning

1. **Tell a Story**: Judges love narratives. Focus on the problem you're solving.
2. **Show Impact**: Use real numbers (items verified, users protected).
3. **Highlight Differentiation**: "Only mobile app" is a powerful differentiator.
4. **Include Press Coverage**: Shows external validation.
5. **Quality Over Quantity**: Apply to fewer awards with excellent applications.
