## 🚨 Backlink Quality Guidelines

### Do Pursue:
✅ Editorial links (mentioned naturally in content)
✅ Resource page links (added to curated lists)
✅ Government/education links (.gov, .edu)
✅ High DA industry publications
✅ Contextual links within relevant content
✅ News mentions and citations

### Don't Pursue:
❌ Paid link schemes (violates Google guidelines)
❌ Link farms or PBNs (private blog networks)
❌ Irrelevant websites (gambling, adult content)
❌ Low-quality directories
❌ Footer/sidebar links (less value)
❌ Reciprocal link exchanges (looks manipulative)

## 💡 Advanced Tactics

### 1. Broken Link Building
- Find broken links on competitor sites
- Reach out: "Hey, noticed this broken link. Our resource covers the same topic"
- Success rate: 5-10%

### 2. Unlinked Brand Mentions
- Use Google Alerts for "SafeOrStolen" mentions
- Reach out: "Thanks for mentioning us! Would you mind linking?"
- Success rate: 30-40%

### 3. HARO (Help A Reporter Out)
- Sign up at helpareporter.com
- Respond to relevant queries daily
- Get quoted in major publications
- Success rate: 2-5% but HIGH quality links

### 4. Digital PR Campaigns
- Create newsworthy content/data
- Pitch to journalists via press releases
- Tie to trending topics or news cycles
- Example: "Stolen goods sales increase 30% during holidays"

### 5. Ego Bait
- Create "Top 50 Security Experts to Follow"
- Include influential people
- They'll share (and often link)
