## Quora Answers

**Question:** How can I tell if a used phone is stolen before buying it?

**Answer:**
Before buying ANY used phone, here's what you need to check:

1. **IMEI Check** - The most reliable method
   - Get the IMEI from the seller (dial *#06# on the phone)
   - Run it through safeorstolen.com - it checks against theft databases
   - Takes 3 seconds, can save you hundreds

2. **Activation Lock** (for iPhones)
   - Make sure they can sign out of iCloud in front of you
   - If they can't, it's likely stolen

3. **Carrier Check**
   - Call the carrier to verify the phone isn't blacklisted
   - Blacklisted = can't be activated

4. **Red flags:**
   - No original box or receipt
   - Price significantly below market
   - Seller rushing the transaction
   - Won't meet at police station

I use safeorstolen.com for every purchase now. Free trial, then affordable plans for frequent buyers.
