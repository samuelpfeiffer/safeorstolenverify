Affiliate Disclosure & Advertising Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

How We Make Money

SafeOrStolen ("we," "us," "our") is committed to transparency about how we generate revenue. Our primary revenue comes from verification subscriptions ($0.99/check or $10/month unlimited). Additionally, when an item is verified as clean, we may recommend complementary third-party services through affiliate partnerships.

What this means for you: Some links on our results pages are affiliate links. If you click on these links and make a purchase or sign up for a service, SafeOrStolen may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These recommendations do not affect your verification results in any way — our verification process is completely independent of our affiliate partnerships.

Our Affiliate Partners

We partner with trusted companies that complement our verification service:

  • Vehicle History Reports — When a vehicle VIN comes back clean, we may recommend vehicle history report services to provide additional ownership, accident, and service records.
  • Identity & Ownership Verification — For additional peace of mind, we may recommend identity verification services to help confirm seller legitimacy.
  • Device Insurance — After verifying an item is not stolen, we may recommend insurance services to protect your purchase.
  • Extended Warranties — For verified electronics and vehicles, we may suggest warranty providers.

We only recommend services that we believe provide genuine value to our users. Our editorial recommendations are not influenced by affiliate compensation.

Geographic Availability

SafeOrStolen's verification services and affiliate partner recommendations are optimized for and primarily serve users in the United States. Our database sources include vehicle theft and title databases, carrier and theft databases, Stolen Firearms Databases, and community theft registries across all 50 US states. Partner service availability may vary by location.
