What the App Can Do

Barcode & QR Scanner

Point your camera at any barcode or QR code to instantly extract and verify the serial number. No typing required — works at gun shows, garage sales, auction houses, and pawn shops.

3-Second Results On-the-Spot

Get full database results before you hand over any money. The app returns Trust Score, risk indicators, and source breakdown — all while you're still looking at the item.

Offline Result Caching

Already checked an item? Results are cached locally. Useful when you're at a venue with spotty signal — check before you lose connection.

Full Database Access

Same 100+ database coverage as the web version — gun serial, IMEI, VIN, license plate, bike serial, and general serial numbers all in one app.

Check History

Every verification is saved in your history. Search, filter, and export your past checks — useful for pawn shops maintaining compliance records.
