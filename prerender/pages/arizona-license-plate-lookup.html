Why Check Before Buying?

Arizona has one of the highest vehicle theft rates in the country, particularly in Phoenix and Tucson metro areas. Vehicles stolen in California often end up in Arizona due to proximity. The Arizona-Mexico border creates additional stolen vehicle trafficking. TheftAZ tracks stolen property across Arizona - always verify before buying a used vehicle in the Grand Canyon State.
