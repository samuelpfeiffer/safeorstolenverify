What Is an IMEI Number and Why Does It Matter?

Every mobile phone manufactured since 2004 has a unique 15-digit IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) number — think of it as the phone's fingerprint. This number is permanently embedded in the device's hardware and is used by carriers worldwide to identify, track, and blacklist devices. When a phone is reported stolen, the carrier adds its IMEI to the GSMA Device Registry — a global blacklist shared across 200+ carriers in 50+ countries. A blacklisted phone cannot be activated on any major carrier network.

This is exactly why checking the IMEI before buying a used phone is the single most important step you can take. Without this check, you could end up with a $1,000+ device that's essentially a paperweight — unable to make calls, send texts, or use cellular data on any major network.

How to Find the IMEI Number on Any Phone

📱 iPhone

  • • Dial *#06#
  • • Settings → General → About → IMEI
  • • Printed on the SIM tray
  • • On the original box barcode

🤖 Android (Samsung, Pixel, etc.)

  • • Dial *#06#
  • • Settings → About Phone → IMEI
  • • Under the battery (older models)
  • • On the original box

⚠️ Warning: If the seller refuses to share the IMEI

This is the single biggest red flag when buying a used phone. An honest seller has nothing to hide. If they won't let you check the IMEI, walk away immediately — the phone is almost certainly stolen, blacklisted, or has an unpaid carrier balance.

Before you meet up or commit to buying, ask the seller for the IMEI number. If they claim they don't know what that is, tell them to dial *#06# — it will display on screen instantly. If they refuse, end the conversation.

For iPhones, go to Settings → [Name] → Find My → Find My iPhone. If Find My is enabled and locked to another Apple ID, the phone cannot be reset or used without the original owner's password. This is separate from the IMEI check and equally critical.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if a phone is stolen before buying it?▼
Get the IMEI number from the seller (dial *#06# on the phone or check Settings → About Phone). Enter it into SafeOrStolen's IMEI checker to cross-reference GSMA, carrier blacklists (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile), and law enforcement databases. Results appear in 3 seconds.
What is an IMEI number and where do I find it?▼
IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) is a unique 15-digit number assigned to every mobile device. Find it by: (1) dialing *#06#, (2) checking Settings → About Phone → IMEI, (3) looking on the original box, or (4) checking the SIM tray on iPhones. Every phone has one, and it cannot be changed without illegal modification.
Can a stolen phone be used after a factory reset?▼
A factory reset does NOT clear a stolen phone's status. The IMEI remains blacklisted across all carriers regardless of resets. iPhones have Activation Lock tied to the Apple ID, and Android phones have Factory Reset Protection (FRP) — both prevent use without the original owner's credentials.
What does a blacklisted IMEI mean?▼
A blacklisted IMEI means the phone was reported as stolen, lost, or has an unpaid financial balance with a carrier. Blacklisted phones cannot be activated on major U.S. carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile). The GSMA maintains a global blacklist shared across 200+ carriers in 50+ countries.
Is it illegal to buy a stolen phone?▼
Yes. In most U.S. states, receiving stolen property is a criminal offense even if you didn't know it was stolen. Under federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2315), knowingly receiving stolen goods worth $5,000+ carries up to 10 years in prison. Law enforcement will seize the phone with no compensation to you.
How much does it cost to check if a phone is stolen?▼
SafeOrStolen offers 2 free checks, then $0.99/check or $10/month unlimited. This is significantly cheaper than CheckMEND (£1.99+/check) and more comprehensive than free-only tools like IMEI.info.
Can I check if an iPhone is stolen without the IMEI?▼
Yes, partially. Check for Activation Lock by going to Settings → [Name] → Find My → Find My iPhone. If it's locked to another Apple ID, the phone may be stolen. However, a full IMEI check against law enforcement databases is the only way to definitively verify theft status.
What should I do if I discover my phone was stolen?▼
Report it immediately to: (1) your carrier to blacklist the IMEI, (2) local police with the IMEI and serial number, (3) your insurance company if applicable, (4) SafeOrStolen's stolen database to alert future buyers. File a police report within 48 hours for best recovery chances.
