How to Check if an iPhone is Stolen Before Buying (2024)

Don't become a victim. Learn how to verify any used iPhone isn't stolen in under 60 seconds - completely free.

Updated: December 2024•5 min read

Why This Matters

1 in 10 used phones sold online is stolen. If you buy a stolen iPhone, you could lose your money AND face legal trouble. Apple can remotely brick stolen phones, leaving you with an expensive paperweight.

Step 1: Get the iPhone's IMEI Number

The IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) is a unique 15-digit number that identifies every phone. Here's how to find it:

📱 On the Phone

  • Dial *#06# - IMEI appears instantly
  • Settings → General → About
  • Check the SIM tray

📦 On the Box

  • Printed on the original box label
  • Compare to the phone's IMEI
  • Mismatched = 🚩 red flag

⚠️ Red Flag:

If the seller refuses to show you the IMEI or won't let you dial *#06#, walk away immediately. Legitimate sellers have nothing to hide.

Step 3: Check Apple's Activation Lock Status

Even if the IMEI is clean, the phone could have Activation Lock enabled. This means:

  • The previous owner didn't sign out of their Apple ID
  • You won't be able to set up the phone
  • This is common with stolen phones

How to check: Go through the setup process. If it asks for an Apple ID you don't know, the phone is locked and potentially stolen.

Step 4: Verify the Seller

Legitimate sellers:

  • ✅ Meet in public places
  • ✅ Let you fully inspect the phone
  • ✅ Provide original receipt or box
  • ✅ Answer questions about the phone's history

Scammers:

  • ❌ Rush the transaction
  • ❌ Price is "too good to be true"
  • ❌ Won't show IMEI
  • ❌ Meet in sketchy locations

What Happens If You Buy a Stolen iPhone?

The Consequences Are Real:

  • Apple can brick it remotely - Your $800 phone becomes worthless
  • No warranty or support - Apple won't help with stolen devices
  • Legal trouble - Possession of stolen property is a crime
  • No refund - Scammers disappear after the sale

The Bottom Line

Checking if an iPhone is stolen takes less than 60 seconds and costs nothing. There's no excuse not to do it before every used phone purchase.
