Why Carfax Works for Cars

Carfax transformed the used car market by answering one simple question: "Is this vehicle safe to buy?"

Before Carfax, buying a used car was risky. You had no idea if the vehicle had been in accidents, had flood damage, or was stolen. Carfax solved this by providing instant VIN verification showing:

  • Accident history
  • Title status (clean, salvage, stolen)
  • Odometer readings
  • Service records
  • Previous owners

This simple concept - instant history verification using a unique identifier (VIN) - became the gold standard for used car transactions. Buyers feel confident. Sellers prove legitimacy. Everyone wins.

How to Check IMEI Like Carfax Checks VIN

Verifying a phone's IMEI with SafeOrStolen works exactly like checking a car's VIN with Carfax:

Step 1: Find the IMEI Number

Just like a VIN uniquely identifies a vehicle, IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) uniquely identifies a phone. Find it by:

  • Dial *#06# on the phone - IMEI displays instantly
  • Go to Settings → About Phone → IMEI Information
  • Check the original box or SIM card tray
  • Look on the back of the phone (some models)

Step 2: Enter IMEI into SafeOrStolen

Visit safeorstolen.com/verify (or use the mobile app) and enter the 15-digit IMEI. You can also scan the barcode on the phone's box using our mobile app - instant scanning, no typing required.

Step 3: Get Instant Results in 3 Seconds

SafeOrStolen checks the IMEI against 150+ databases including:

  • carrier and theft databases Device Check - Global IMEI blacklist (carrier-reported stolen phones)
  • FBI/stolen property reports from all 50 states Database - Law enforcement stolen property reports
  • Insurance Claims - Phones reported stolen to insurance companies
  • Carrier Blacklists - AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile stolen device databases
  • International Registries - Interpol and global stolen phone databases

Step 4: Review the Verification Report

Like a Carfax report shows "Clean Title" or "Salvage Title," SafeOrStolen shows:

  • ✅

    SAFE TO BUY

    Not reported stolen. Clean IMEI. No theft records found across 150+ databases.

  • 🚨

    STOLEN

    Device reported stolen. Found in law enforcement/carrier databases. DO NOT PURCHASE.

  • ⚠️

    CAUTION

    Mixed signals. Some databases show issues. Verify with seller before proceeding.
