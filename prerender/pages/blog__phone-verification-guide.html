Complete Phone Verification Guide: IMEI Check & Stolen Phone Lookup

Why Phone Verification Matters

Over 2 million smartphones are stolen in the United States every year, with many ending up for sale on secondhand marketplaces. Buying a stolen phone isn't just financially risky—it's potentially illegal and can lead to your device being remotely locked or seized by authorities.

Whether you're buying from eBay, Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, or Craigslist, verifying a phone's IMEI number is your most powerful protection against fraud. This comprehensive guide teaches you everything you need to know about phone verification.

What Is an IMEI Number?

IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) is a unique 15-digit code assigned to every mobile phone. Think of it as your phone's fingerprint—no two devices share the same IMEI.

Format: 123456789012345

How to Find Your IMEI Number

Method 1: Dial Code (Works on All Phones)

Dial *#06# on your phone's keypad. The IMEI will display instantly.

Method 2: Settings Menu

iPhone: Settings → General → About → IMEI

Android: Settings → About Phone → Status → IMEI

Method 3: Physical Label

Check the SIM card tray or under the battery (older models). Also printed on original packaging.

Red Flags When Buying Used Phones

Seller Refuses to Provide IMEI

Legitimate sellers have no reason to hide IMEI numbers. Refusal indicates the phone is likely stolen or blacklisted.

Price Significantly Below Market Value

A brand new iPhone for 50% off retail is almost certainly stolen. Check current market prices before buying.

Find My iPhone Still Enabled

If Find My iPhone or Android Device Protection is active, the previous owner can remotely lock or erase the phone at any time.

No Original Packaging or Receipt

Stolen phones rarely come with boxes or proof of purchase. Ask for documentation before buying.

Seller Has Multiple Identical Phones

Multiple iPhones or Samsung phones for sale simultaneously suggests organized theft operations.

Cash Only, Quick Sale Pressure

Scammers demand cash and create urgency to prevent verification. Take your time and verify before paying.

What Happens If You Buy a Stolen Phone?

If you unknowingly purchase a stolen phone, you face multiple serious consequences:

  • Criminal Charges: You can be charged with possession of stolen property even if you purchased it unknowingly
  • Device Blacklisting: Carriers will blacklist the IMEI, making the phone unusable on any network
  • Remote Lock/Erase: The original owner can use Find My iPhone to lock or wipe your device
  • Financial Loss: You lose both the phone and your money with no legal recourse against the seller
  • Seizure by Police: Authorities can confiscate the device as evidence
