2. IMEI.info — Best Free Phone-Only Checker

imei.info · Free unlimited · Phones only

IMEI.info is a popular free service for checking phone IMEI numbers against carrier blacklists. It provides basic device information including model, storage, color, and blacklist status. It's fast, free, and unlimited — making it a solid choice for phone-only verification.

Strengths

  • Free and unlimited
  • Device specs included
  • Fast results

Limitations

  • Phones only (no cars, guns, etc.)
  • ~10 databases (vs 100+)
  • No mobile app
  • No police database access

Best for: Quick phone blacklist checks when you only need carrier status. For comprehensive stolen property verification including police databases, SafeOrStolen's IMEI checker searches more sources.

3. Carfax — Best Detailed Vehicle History

carfax.com · $44.99/report · Vehicles only

Carfax is the industry standard for detailed vehicle history reports including accident history, ownership chain, mileage verification, service records, and theft status. It's the most comprehensive option for vehicles — but it's expensive and vehicles-only.

Strengths

  • Most detailed vehicle history
  • Accident and service records
  • Industry-trusted brand

Limitations

  • $44.99 per report (expensive)
  • Vehicles only
  • No mobile app for quick checks
  • No phones, firearms, electronics

Best for: Buyers who need full vehicle history (accidents, service, ownership). If you only need theft verification, SafeOrStolen's VIN checker provides comparable theft data at approximately 98% less cost ($10/mo vs $44.99/report).

4. CheckMEND (Recipero) — Best for UK Buyers

checkmend.com · £7/check · Phones and electronics

CheckMEND, powered by Recipero, specializes in device verification primarily for the UK market. It checks phones and electronics against UK police databases, insurance records, and carrier blacklists. It's the standard verification tool for UK pawn shops and secondhand retailers.

Strengths

  • Strong UK police data
  • UK pawn shop standard
  • Insurance company partnerships

Limitations

  • £7 per check (expensive)
  • UK-focused data (limited US coverage)
  • No firearms or vehicles
  • No mobile app

Best for: UK-based buyers and pawn shops. For US buyers, SafeOrStolen provides better coverage of American databases at lower cost.

5. Swappa — Best Phone Marketplace with Built-In Checks

swappa.com · Free (10/day limit) · Phones only

Swappa is a phone resale marketplace that includes built-in IMEI verification. When buying through Swappa, devices are automatically checked against carrier blacklists before listing. Their standalone ESN/IMEI checker is available for free with a 10-check daily limit.

Strengths

  • Free with marketplace
  • Built into purchase flow
  • Buyer protection program

Limitations

  • 10 checks/day limit
  • Phones only
  • Carrier blacklists only
  • No police database access

Best for: Buyers already shopping on Swappa. For items from other marketplaces (Facebook, OfferUp, Craigslist), use SafeOrStolen's phone check for more comprehensive results.

6. NICB VINCheck — Best Free Vehicle Theft Check

nicb.org · Free (5/day) · Vehicles only

NICB (National Insurance Crime Bureau) offers a free VINCheck service that searches insurance theft and salvage/junk records. It's backed by the insurance industry and provides reliable data on whether a vehicle has been reported stolen through insurance claims. Limited to 5 searches per day.

Strengths

  • Free
  • Insurance industry backed
  • Reliable theft data

Limitations

  • 5 searches per day
  • Vehicles only
  • Only insurance claims (not police)
  • No app

Best for: Quick free vehicle theft checks. For comprehensive coverage including NHTSA vehicle records and police databases, use SafeOrStolen's VIN checker.

7. 50-State Firearm Reports — Best Free Firearm Serial Lookup

the national stolen firearm registry · Free unlimited · Firearms only

50-State Firearm Reports is a community-driven stolen firearm database where gun owners can report and search for stolen weapons. It contains approximately 32,000 user-reported stolen guns. Free and unlimited, but relies entirely on voluntary community reporting — it does not access official community theft registries like stolen property reports from all 50 states.

Strengths

  • Free and unlimited
  • Simple interface
  • Community-driven

Limitations

  • Only 32K entries (vs stolen property reports from all 50 states's 7M+)
  • Community reports only (no police data)
  • Firearms only
  • No app

Best for: Quick free gun serial checks as a first step. For firearm checks alongside every other item category, use SafeOrStolen's gun serial number lookup (2 free checks).

8. Local Police Department — Most Authoritative

Your local PD · Free · Any item type

Your local police department can check any serial number against stolen property reports from all 50 states — the most authoritative stolen property database in the country. This is free and works for any item type. The downside: it requires an in-person visit, and results can take 3-7 business days.

Strengths

  • Official stolen property reports from all 50 states
  • Free
  • Any item type
  • Most authoritative source

Limitations

  • In-person visit required
  • 3-7 day wait time
  • Not practical for marketplace meetups
  • No app

Best for: High-value purchases where you can wait days for results. For instant verification at gun shows, marketplace meetups, and pawn shops, use SafeOrStolen for 3-second results.

9. BikeIndex — Best for Bicycle Theft

bikeindex.org · Free · Bicycles only

BikeIndex is a nonprofit bicycle registration and stolen bike database with over 1.3 million registered bikes. It partners with police departments in many US cities and has recovered over 13,000 stolen bicycles. Free to register and search.

Strengths

  • Free nonprofit
  • Police partnerships
  • 13K+ bikes recovered

Limitations

  • Bicycles only
  • Voluntary registration
  • No app

Best for: Bicycle buyers. SafeOrStolen also includes BikeIndex data in our bicycle verification along with additional police and insurance databases.

10. eBay's Built-In Verification — Best for eBay Shoppers

ebay.com · Built into platform · eBay listings only

eBay has internal fraud detection and buyer protection that can identify some stolen goods. Their Money Back Guarantee covers most purchases. However, eBay's systems don't check external stolen property databases — they primarily rely on seller reputation and internal fraud patterns.

Strengths

  • Built into purchases
  • Buyer protection
  • Seller reputation system

Limitations

  • eBay only
  • No external stolen databases
  • Reactive (after purchase)
  • No serial number verification

Best for: Passive protection on eBay purchases. For proactive verification before buying, use SafeOrStolen's eBay check to verify serial numbers before bidding.
