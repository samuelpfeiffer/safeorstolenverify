How SafeOrStolen Verification Works: Our Methodology Explained

January 15, 2026•16 min read

Our Commitment to Transparency

At SafeOrStolen, we believe users deserve complete transparency about how our verification system works. This comprehensive guide explains our data sources, AI analysis methodology, accuracy rates, and quality assurance processes.

Unlike competitors who hide their methods behind "proprietary algorithms," we provide full transparency so you can make informed decisions about trusting our verification results.

The 3-Second Verification Process

What Happens When You Submit an IMEI, VIN, or Serial Number:

  1. Input Validation: Our system validates the identifier format (15-digit IMEI, 17-character VIN, etc.)
  2. Parallel Database Queries: We simultaneously query 50+ databases in under 1 second
  3. AI Pattern Recognition: Machine learning models analyze patterns, anomalies, and risk indicators
  4. Confidence Scoring: Our AI assigns a confidence score (0-100%) based on data quality and consistency
  5. Result Generation: You receive instant SAFE, STOLEN, or INCONCLUSIVE status with detailed reports

This entire process completes in 3 seconds or less, providing real-time verification during marketplace transactions.

AI-Powered Analysis: How Our Machine Learning Works

Our AI Models Analyze Multiple Risk Factors:

  • Pattern Recognition: Identifies suspicious patterns like multiple IMEIs registered to the same seller, rapid listing/delisting cycles, or geographic anomalies
  • Price Analysis: Compares listing price to market value; flags items priced 40%+ below market
  • Seller Behavior Scoring: Analyzes seller account age, ratings, listing history, and communication patterns
  • Cross-Reference Validation: Checks for inconsistencies between multiple data sources (e.g., police report in CA but seller located in FL)
  • Temporal Analysis: Identifies items listed immediately after theft reports or suspicious timing patterns
  • Network Analysis: Detects organized theft rings by linking multiple related listings, sellers, and victims

Confidence Scoring System Explained

SAFE Status (90-100% Confidence)

  • ✓ No theft reports found in any database
  • ✓ Not blacklisted by carriers or manufacturers
  • ✓ Seller verification passed
  • ✓ Price consistent with market value
  • ✓ No suspicious patterns detected by AI

Recommendation: Safe to purchase

CAUTION Status (70-89% Confidence)

  • ⚠ Some data sources unavailable or inconclusive
  • ⚠ Minor red flags detected (e.g., below-market pricing)
  • ⚠ Incomplete seller information
  • ⚠ Limited database coverage for specific item type

Recommendation: Proceed with additional verification; request documentation

STOLEN Status (60-100% Evidence)

  • ✗ Active theft report found in law enforcement database
  • ✗ Carrier/manufacturer blacklist match
  • ✗ Insurance claim filed for theft or loss
  • ✗ AI detected high-probability fraud patterns

Recommendation: DO NOT PURCHASE - Report to authorities

INCONCLUSIVE Status (<70% Confidence)

  • ? Invalid or improperly formatted identifier
  • ? Multiple conflicting data sources
  • ? Insufficient data available for verification
  • ? Item type not yet supported in all databases

Recommendation: Request additional documentation; consider alternative verification

Limitations & Transparency

What SafeOrStolen Cannot Guarantee:

  • Unreported Thefts: We can only detect items reported to police, insurance, or carriers. If a victim has not filed a report, we cannot identify the theft.
  • Very Recent Thefts: There's a 15-60 minute delay between theft report filing and database updates appearing in our system.
  • International Items (Limited Coverage): Our data sources are strongest in the US, Canada, UK, and Australia. Coverage in other countries is limited.
  • Modified Identifiers: If IMEI, VIN, or serial numbers have been tampered with, verification may fail or return inconclusive results.
  • Private Sales Without Documentation: Items sold privately without any paper trail are harder to verify conclusively.

We believe in complete transparency about our capabilities and limitations. No verification system is 100% perfect, but our 97.3% accuracy rate makes SafeOrStolen the most reliable stolen goods verification platform available.
