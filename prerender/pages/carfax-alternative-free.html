Looking for a free Carfax alternative? SafeOrStolen offers free stolen vehicle verification while Carfax charges $44.99 per report.

Understanding the Difference

Important: SafeOrStolen and Carfax serve different purposes:

🔒 SafeOrStolen (FREE)

Verifies if a vehicle is stolen. Checks NICB, theft reports, insurance claims. Essential before any purchase.

📋 Carfax ($44.99)

Shows vehicle history: accidents, service records, ownership, odometer. Useful for understanding condition.

Best practice: Use SafeOrStolen (free) to verify the vehicle isn't stolen FIRST, then consider Carfax for full history if the theft check is clean.
