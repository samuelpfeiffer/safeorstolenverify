What is Android IMEI Verification?

Every Android phone has a unique 15-digit IMEI number. When stolen, this IMEI gets reported to carrier and theft databases and stolen device databases. A blacklisted Android phone won't work on major carrier networks. Our verification checks Google's Find My Device reports, carrier and theft databases, theft reports, and insurance claims to determine if an Android phone is safe to purchase.

Android phones are frequently stolen due to their widespread use and resale value. Google Pixel and OnePlus devices are particularly targeted. Protect yourself from buying stolen property.
