What is Firearm Serial Number Verification?

Every firearm manufactured after 1968 has a unique serial number. When stolen, firearms are reported by police to the FBI' Buying a stolen firearm, even unknowingly, can result in federal charges. Our check queries stolen firearms databases and analyzes the serial number for signs of alteration or obliteration.

Why Verify Firearms Before Buying?

Over 380,000 firearms are stolen annually in the US. Possessing a stolen firearm is a federal crime, even if you didn't know it was stolen. The legal consequences are severe.
