What is Firearm Serial Number Verification?

Every firearm manufactured after 1968 has a unique serial number. When stolen, firearms are reported by police to the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC), which is restricted to law enforcement and cannot be queried by us or any other consumer service. Law enforcement traces firearms by serial number. Buying a stolen firearm, even unknowingly, can result in federal charges. Our verification checks available databases to help you avoid purchasing stolen firearms.
