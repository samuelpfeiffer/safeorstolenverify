What is Any Item Verification?

Checking if an item is stolen means verifying the item hasn't been reported as lost, stolen, or fraudulently obtained. Every valuable item has a unique identifier - IMEI for phones, VIN for vehicles, serial numbers for electronics, power tools, and appliances. SafeOrStolen checks these identifiers against community theft registries, carrier and theft databases, insurance claims, and user-reported theft registries to determine if an item is safe to buy.

Why Check Before Buying?

Buying stolen property can result in losing your money AND facing criminal charges. You have no legal ownership of stolen goods - they can be confiscated by police at any time with zero compensation. Phones get blacklisted and become useless. Cars get impounded. Electronics get seized. Protect yourself with a verification before any secondhand purchase.
