What is Jewelry Verification?

Many valuable jewelry pieces have identifying marks: diamond laser inscriptions (GIA, AGS), hallmarks, engraved serial numbers, or unique identifiers. When stolen, these identifying numbers are reported to police, insurance companies, and databases like The Jewelry Security Alliance. Our verification searches these sources to help protect you from purchasing stolen property.

Why Check Jewelry Before Buying?

Jewelry theft costs billions annually. Stolen pieces often end up in pawn shops, estate sales, and online marketplaces. Protect yourself from legal and financial consequences.

GIA-inscribed diamonds can be traced and identified
Insurance companies aggressively pursue stolen jewelry recovery
Police use inscriptions and photos to match recovered jewelry
Possession of stolen property leads to confiscation and possible charges
No legal claim to stolen jewelry regardless of what you paid
Expensive jewelry is often photographed and documented by owners
Pawn shops check databases before purchasing
High-value pieces should always have provenance documentation
