What is Motorcycle VIN Verification?

Every motorcycle has a 17-character VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) stamped on the frame. When stolen, this VIN is reported to vehicle theft and title databases, and community theft registries. Motorcycles are stolen at nearly twice the rate of cars, making verification essential. Our service checks these databases to ensure you're not buying stolen property.
