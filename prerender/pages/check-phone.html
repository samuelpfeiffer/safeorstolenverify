How to find the IMEI

  • Dial *#06# on the phone — the IMEI appears instantly
  • Settings → About Phone (Android) or Settings → General → About (iPhone)
  • Printed on the SIM tray, original box, or purchase receipt

Ask the seller for the IMEI before you meet. A legitimate seller sends it in under a minute. A seller who refuses, stalls, or sends a photo of a different device just answered your real question.

What we check

SafeOrStolen queries stolen-device and blacklist records across more than 100 databases, including carrier blacklist status, loss and theft reports, and our own user-submitted theft registry. A result comes back in seconds:

  • Clean — no loss, theft, or blacklist records found for this identifier
  • Flagged — the device has been reported lost, stolen, or blacklisted. Walk away.

What a "clean" result means — honestly

No single database catches everything. A clean result means no report exists in the sources we check at this moment — it is strong evidence, not a legal guarantee. That's why we also recommend meeting in a police-station safe-exchange zone and getting a written receipt with the seller's name and the IMEI on it. Those two habits, plus a clean check, remove nearly all of the risk.
