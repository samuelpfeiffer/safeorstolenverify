What is Samsung IMEI Verification?

Every Samsung Galaxy phone has a unique 15-digit IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) number. When a Samsung device is reported stolen, lost, or has unpaid financing, the IMEI gets added to carrier and theft databases and global stolen device databases. Our verification service instantly checks your Samsung's IMEI against carrier and theft databases, and law enforcement records. A blacklisted Samsung won't work on cellular networks, making it essentially worthless.
