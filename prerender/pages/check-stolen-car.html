Where do I find the VIN number?

The VIN is located on the dashboard (visible through windshield), driver's door jamb, vehicle registration, and insurance documents.

What if the VIN shows as stolen?

Do not proceed with the purchase. Contact local law enforcement immediately. Never confront the seller yourself.
