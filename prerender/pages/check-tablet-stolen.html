What is Tablet Serial Number Verification?

Every tablet has a unique serial number. iPads can be tracked via Find My iPad and locked with Activation Lock. Samsung tablets have similar protections. When tablets are reported stolen, serial numbers are logged in manufacturer databases, law enforcement records, and insurance claims. Our verification checks these sources to protect you from buying stolen property.
