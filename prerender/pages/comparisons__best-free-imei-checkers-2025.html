Frequently Asked Questions

Are free IMEI checkers reliable?

It depends on the service. SafeOrStolen offers genuinely free blacklist checks backed by law enforcement data. Many others charge for this feature or use limited databases.

What's the difference between blacklist and stolen check?

Blacklist refers to phones blocked by carriers (unpaid bills, fraud). Stolen check looks for devices reported to police or insurance. The best services check both.

How do I find my IMEI number?

Dial *#06# on any phone to display the IMEI. You can also find it in Settings → About Phone, or printed on the original box.

Why do some services charge for blacklist checks?

Accessing carrier and law enforcement databases has costs. Some services pass this to users per-check, while others like SafeOrStolen include it free with limits.
