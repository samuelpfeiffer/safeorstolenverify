Frequently Asked Questions

Is NICB VINCheck free?

Yes, NICB VINCheck is completely free but limited to 5 searches per day and only checks vehicles against insurance theft and salvage records.

Why choose SafeOrStolen over NICB?

SafeOrStolen has a larger database (100+ items), supports more item types (phones, bikes, tools), offers a mobile app, and provides instant results. NICB only checks vehicles and can be slow.

Can I use NICB VINCheck for phones?

No, NICB VINCheck only works for vehicle VINs. To check phone IMEIs, use SafeOrStolen which supports both VIN and IMEI checks.

Does SafeOrStolen have salvage title checks like NICB?

Yes, SafeOrStolen's comprehensive database includes salvage, flood, and rebuilt title information from insurance companies, similar to NICB.
