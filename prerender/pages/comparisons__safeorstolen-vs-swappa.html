Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Swappa's ESN check for Facebook Marketplace items?

No, Swappa's ESN check only works for devices listed on their platform. To check items from other marketplaces, use SafeOrStolen.

Does SafeOrStolen check more than just phones?

Yes! SafeOrStolen verifies phones (IMEI), cars and motorcycles (VIN), and any item with a serial number - bikes, tools, laptops, cameras, and more.

Which has a bigger stolen item database?

SafeOrStolen has 100+ verified stolen items from law enforcement and insurance partners. Swappa performs basic carrier ESN checks but doesn't have access to community theft registries.

Should I use both services?

Yes! They serve different purposes. Use Swappa's marketplace for buying/selling with payment protection, and SafeOrStolen for comprehensive stolen verification on any platform.
