Frequently Asked Questions

Is a free VIN check reliable?

Free VIN checks from reputable sources like SafeOrStolen and NICB VINCheck are reliable for stolen vehicle verification. For complete history, paid reports are more comprehensive.

Do I need both a stolen check and Carfax?

We recommend both. SafeOrStolen's free check verifies the car isn't stolen before you invest in a full Carfax report. It saves money and time on potentially fraudulent listings.

What can SafeOrStolen check that Carfax can't?

SafeOrStolen checks a 100+ stolen item database from law enforcement and insurance companies. It also checks phones, bikes, tools, and other items - not just vehicles.

Where is the VIN located?

The VIN is typically on the driver's side dashboard (visible through windshield), the driver's door jamb, or the vehicle registration/title documents.
