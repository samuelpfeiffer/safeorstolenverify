About SafeOrStolen

SafeOrStolen is the "Carfax for Everything" — a universal verification platform for the secondhand market. The platform helps buyers avoid purchasing stolen goods and helps sellers prove their items are legitimate.

  • IMEI Verification — Smartphones via carrier and theft databases
  • VIN Lookup — Cars, trucks, motorcycles via vehicle theft and title databases
  • Serial Number Verification — Electronics, tools, equipment
  • Firearm Check — stolen firearms databases + serial integrity analysis
  • License Plate Lookup — All 50 US states
