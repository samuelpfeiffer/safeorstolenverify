Most Common Facebook Marketplace Scams

1. Stolen Phone Scam

Phone looks perfect but has a blacklisted IMEI. It won't activate on any carrier. The seller knows this and disappears after sale.

Prevention: Always check IMEI with SafeOrStolen before buying.

2. Salvage Title Vehicle

Car was totaled and rebuilt. Seller hides this and claims clean title. May have hidden damage.

Prevention: Run VIN check with SafeOrStolen to reveal title history.

3. Counterfeit Electronics

Fake AirPods, counterfeit gaming consoles, knockoff gadgets sold as authentic.

Prevention: Verify serial numbers match manufacturer records.

4. Advance Payment Scam

Seller requests deposit or full payment before meeting. Disappears with your money.

Prevention: Never pay before inspecting item in person.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Facebook Marketplace safe?

Facebook Marketplace can be safe with precautions. Verify items with SafeOrStolen, meet in public, use Facebook Pay, check profiles, and never wire money. About 1 in 5 listings may be scams.

How do I avoid scams on Facebook Marketplace?

1) Verify IMEI/VIN numbers with SafeOrStolen, 2) Meet at police stations, 3) Never pay with gift cards or wire transfers, 4) Check seller profile age, 5) Trust your instincts about prices.

How do I check if a phone on Marketplace is stolen?

Ask for the IMEI number (dial *#06#). Enter it into SafeOrStolen before meeting. If seller refuses to provide IMEI, do not proceed.
