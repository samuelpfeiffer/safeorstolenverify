The Hidden Danger of Facebook Marketplace

Every day, thousands of stolen items are listed on Facebook Marketplace. Without verification, you could unknowingly become a victim.

Stolen Phones

iPhones and Samsung devices are the most commonly stolen items sold on Facebook Marketplace. A stolen phone can be blacklisted, making it unusable on any carrier.

Stolen Vehicles

Cars, motorcycles, and boats with cloned or altered VINs appear legitimate until police recover them. You'll lose the vehicle AND your money.

Stolen Electronics

Laptops, tablets, gaming consoles, and cameras are frequently stolen and resold. Serial numbers can reveal if the item was reported stolen.

Red Flags on Facebook Marketplace Listings

Price Too Good to Be True

Stolen items are often priced 40-60% below market value for quick sales

Seller Won't Provide Serial Number

Legitimate sellers have nothing to hide and will share identifiers

New Account with No History

Thieves create fresh accounts to avoid being traced

Rushing the Sale

Pressure to meet immediately or 'another buyer waiting' is suspicious

Won't Meet in Public

Insisting on private locations may indicate stolen goods

Cash Only, No Records

Avoiding any paper trail is a common thief tactic

Missing Original Packaging

No box, receipt, or accessories could indicate theft

Factory Reset Already Done

Pre-wiped devices may be hiding activation locks

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if an item on Facebook Marketplace is stolen?

Use SafeOrStolen's free verification tool. Enter the item's IMEI (for phones), VIN (for vehicles), or serial number (for electronics) to instantly check against stolen property databases before meeting the seller.

Can I verify items before meeting a Facebook Marketplace seller?

Yes! Download the SafeOrStolen app to verify items instantly at meetup locations. Our mobile app works offline and can scan barcodes in just 3 seconds.

What types of items can I verify on Facebook Marketplace?

SafeOrStolen verifies phones (IMEI), vehicles (VIN), laptops, electronics, bikes, tools, firearms, boats, jewelry, and appliances using serial numbers.

Is it illegal to buy stolen items on Facebook Marketplace?

Yes. In most jurisdictions, receiving stolen property is a crime even if you didn't know it was stolen. You can face criminal charges and will lose the item when recovered by police.

How common are stolen items on Facebook Marketplace?

Studies show that 1 in 5 secondhand electronics may have a questionable history. Facebook Marketplace's ease of use makes it a target for stolen goods resale.

What should I do if I discover an item is stolen?

Do not complete the purchase. Report the listing to Facebook and contact local police. Never confront the seller directly about stolen property.
