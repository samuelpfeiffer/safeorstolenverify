Frequently Asked Questions

How does verification detect fraud?

Our system can identify if an item was reported stolen before the claimed date, if the same item appears in multiple claims, or if the serial numbers don't match claimed devices.

Can you help with recovery matching?

Yes! When police recover stolen items, we can match serial numbers against your claim database to identify items your company insured.

What data sources do you check?

We check community theft registries, carrier and theft databases, manufacturer records, and a network of insurance company reports totaling 50M+ records.

Is the API suitable for high-volume claims?

Absolutely. Our enterprise API is built for high volume, with 24/7 availability and dedicated support.
