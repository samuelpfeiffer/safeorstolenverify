Challenges Facing Law Enforcement

The secondhand market is growing, but so are the risks. Here's what law enforcements face daily.

Property Identification

Recovered items without clear ownership indicators are difficult to return to victims.

Database Fragmentation

Stolen property is reported to many different databases. Searching each is time-consuming.

Cross-Jurisdiction Cases

Items stolen in one jurisdiction often surface in another, making tracking difficult.

Frequently Asked Questions

What databases do you search?

We aggregate data from carrier and theft databases, manufacturer stolen device registries, insurance company reports, and user-reported theft databases totaling 100+ databases.

Can officers use this in the field?

Yes! Our mobile app and responsive website work on any smartphone or MDT, allowing instant verification during traffic stops or investigations.

How do you help return property to owners?

When property is registered with us and later recovered, we can facilitate connection between law enforcement and the rightful owner.

Is there special pricing for law enforcement?

Yes, we offer special rates for police departments and law enforcement agencies. Contact us for department pricing.
