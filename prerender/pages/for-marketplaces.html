Frequently Asked Questions

How does the marketplace API integration work?

Our REST API accepts IMEI, VIN, or serial numbers and returns verification results in JSON format within 3 seconds. You can integrate at listing creation, during checkout, or as a standalone verification tool for buyers.

What databases does the API search?

We search 100+ databases including carrier blacklists (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile), law enforcement stolen property records, insurance company claims, manufacturer registries, and pawn shop databases totaling 50M+ records.

Can we add verified badges to listings?

Yes! When a seller verifies an item through our API, you can display a 'Verified Clean' badge on the listing. This builds buyer trust and increases engagement by up to 40%.

What is the API pricing for marketplaces?

We offer volume-based pricing for marketplaces starting at competitive per-check rates. Contact partnerships@safeorstolen.com for custom enterprise pricing based on your verification volume.
