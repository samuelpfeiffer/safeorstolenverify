Compliance with State Pawn Laws

Most states mandate pawn shops verify items against community theft registries before acceptance:

  • California (CA Business & Professions Code §21628): Requires electronic reporting and verification
  • Texas (TX Occupations Code §371.151): Pawnbrokers must check against stolen property databases
  • Florida (FL Statute §539.001): Requires verification through state law enforcement systems
  • New York (NY General Business Law §45): Mandates daily reporting and verification

SafeOrStolen automatically fulfills these requirements with instant verification and compliance documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is verification at the counter?

3 seconds average. Scan the barcode or type the serial number, and results display instantly.

What if an item doesn't have a serial number?

We recommend photographing the item and customer ID. For high-value items without serials (jewelry), consider requiring additional documentation or declining the transaction.

Do you provide compliance reports for regulators?

Yes. Business plans include automated monthly summaries showing all verifications performed, compliant with state reporting requirements.

What happens if we accept a stolen item despite verification?

Your verification record proves due diligence, significantly reducing legal liability. In most cases, prosecutors won't charge pawn shops that performed proper verification.

Can we verify items before customers arrive?

Yes. If a customer texts you serial numbers, you can pre-verify and decline before they come in, saving time.

Do you offer training for our staff?

Business plans include onboarding training and ongoing support. We also provide training materials you can use for new hires.
