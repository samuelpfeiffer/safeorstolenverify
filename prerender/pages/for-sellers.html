Seller FAQ

How does the "Verified Safe" badge work?▼
After verification, you receive a unique link (e.g., safeorstolen.com/verify/abc123) and a downloadable PDF certificate. Add the link to your listing or include the badge image. Buyers can click to see the full verified report — no signup required.
What information does the buyer see?▼
Buyers see the verification result (CLEAN or FLAGGED), the Trust Score (0-100), which databases were checked, and the verification date. For privacy, only the last 4 digits of the identifier are shown. Your personal info is never shared.
Does verification guarantee my item isn't stolen?▼
No verification service can provide a 100% guarantee because not all thefts are reported to databases. However, checking 100+ databases provides the most comprehensive available verification and demonstrates documented due diligence.
Can I verify items in bulk?▼
Yes! Verified Seller and Premium members can upload a CSV file with multiple serial numbers and receive batch verification reports. Perfect for resellers, estate sale companies, and high-volume sellers.
How long are verification reports valid?▼
Reports are valid for 90 days from the verification date. After that, we recommend re-verifying to ensure databases haven't been updated with new theft reports.
What if my item comes back as FLAGGED?▼
Do not sell the item. Contact your local police with the verification report. If you purchased the item legitimately, file a police report and contact the original seller. Our report provides the evidence law enforcement needs.
Where can I use the badge?▼
Anywhere you sell: Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist, OfferUp, Mercari, Poshmark, Swappa, and any other platform. The badge is an image and the report is a URL — works everywhere.
What platforms accept verified badges?▼
All of them. The badge is a standard image file and the verification report is a URL. Any platform that allows images or links in listings supports SafeOrStolen badges.
What's included in the plan?▼
$10/month gets you unlimited verifications, unlimited badges, seller profile, verification history, PDF certificates, and priority support. Cancel anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?▼
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard anytime. Your subscription continues until the end of the billing period. No cancellation fees.
