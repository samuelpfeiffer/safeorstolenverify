How to Verify eBay Items Before Buying

Protect yourself from purchasing stolen goods on eBay. Use SafeOrStolen's free verification tools to check any item's history before you bid.

Why You Should Verify eBay Purchases

Stolen Goods Risk

eBay's massive marketplace makes it a target for fencing stolen items, especially electronics and phones.

Legal Protection

Unknowingly buying stolen property can result in the item being seized with no refund.

Peace of Mind

Verification takes 3 seconds and gives you confidence in your purchase.

Step-by-Step Verification Guide

1

Ask the Seller for the Identifier

Before bidding, message the seller and request:

  • Phones: IMEI number (dial *#06# on the device)
  • Vehicles: VIN (17-character code on dashboard or door)
  • Electronics: Serial number (usually on bottom or in settings)
  • Bikes: Serial number (under bottom bracket)
2

Verify with SafeOrStolen

Enter the identifier into our free verification tool. We check 100+ databases including:

  • Stolen property reports from all 50 states (FBI stolen property database)
  • vehicle theft and title databases
  • Carrier blacklists (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile)
  • International stolen databases
3

Check for Red Flags

Be wary of:

  • Prices significantly below market value
  • Sellers who refuse to provide identifiers
  • Stock photos instead of actual item photos
  • New accounts with no feedback
  • Pressure to complete sale quickly or outside eBay
4

Use eBay's Secure Payment

Always pay through eBay's official checkout for buyer protection. Never pay via wire transfer, gift cards, or outside eBay.
