What is Firearm Verification?

A gun serial number lookup verifies whether a firearm's unique identifier has been reported as stolen, lost, or associated with criminal activity. Every firearm manufactured since 1968 is required to have a serial number, and this number is recorded when a gun is reported stolen. Our gun serial number lookup checks stolen firearms databases and analyzes the serial number for signs of alteration or obliteration.

Why Check Before Buying?

Buying or possessing a stolen firearm is a federal crime under 18 U.S. Code § 922(j), punishable by up to 10 years imprisonment. There is no 'I didn't know' defense - if the gun is stolen, you face criminal prosecution. Private sales, gun shows, and online transactions are where most stolen guns circulate because no background check is required in many states. A free gun serial number lookup takes 3 seconds and could save you from: federal felony charges, confiscation of all your firearms, permanent loss of gun rights, and financial loss with no recourse.
