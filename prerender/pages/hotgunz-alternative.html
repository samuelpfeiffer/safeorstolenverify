What is HotGunz?

HotGunz is a free public database of stolen firearms with over 32,000 user-reported entries. Victims report their stolen guns directly, often before or in addition to police reports. It's a valuable resource, but it's just ONE database.

SafeOrStolen includes HotGunz data

When you search a firearm serial on SafeOrStolen, we query the HotGunz national stolen-firearms registry and analyze the serial for signs of alteration or obliteration. Federal systems (federal law-enforcement systems, ATF eTrace) are restricted to law enforcement and are not checked by us or by any other consumer service.

Why One Registry Is Never Enough

A stolen gun might be reported to police but never submitted to a public registry like HotGunz. Public sources only contain what victims and departments choose to share, so a clean result narrows the risk — it does not clear the firearm.
