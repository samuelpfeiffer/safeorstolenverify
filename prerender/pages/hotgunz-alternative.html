What is HotGunz?

HotGunz is a free public database of stolen firearms with over 32,000 user-reported entries. Victims report their stolen guns directly, often before or in addition to police reports. It's a valuable resource, but it's just ONE database.

SafeOrStolen includes HotGunz data

When you search a firearm serial on SafeOrStolen, we query the HotGunz national stolen-firearms registry and analyze the serial for signs of alteration or obliteration. Federal systems (FBI NCIC, ATF eTrace) are restricted to law enforcement and are not checked by us or by any other consumer service.
