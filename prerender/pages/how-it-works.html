How to Find Identifiers

📱 IMEI (Phone/Tablet)

Method 1: Dial *#06# on your phone

Method 2: Settings → About Phone → IMEI

Method 3: Check the original box or SIM tray

IMEI is 15 digits long

🚗 VIN (Vehicle)

Method 1: Dashboard near windshield (driver's side)

Method 2: Driver's side door jamb sticker

Method 3: Vehicle registration or insurance card

VIN is 17 characters long

🔢 Serial Number (Electronics/Other)

Method 1: Check device label or sticker

Method 2: Settings → About → Serial Number

Method 3: Original packaging or receipt

Serial numbers vary in length
