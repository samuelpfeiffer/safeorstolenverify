Step-by-Step: Check if a Phone is Stolen

1

Get the Phone's IMEI Number

Before meeting the seller, ask for the phone's IMEI number. This is a unique 15-digit identifier for every phone. There are several ways to find it:

Dial *#06#

Works on any phone - displays IMEI instantly

Phone Settings

iPhone: Settings > General > About

Original Box

Printed on the barcode label

Red Flag: If the seller refuses to provide the IMEI, do not proceed with the purchase. This is a major warning sign.

Review Verification Results

4

Check for Activation Locks

Even if the IMEI is clean, check for activation locks that could prevent you from using the phone:

iPhone (iCloud Lock)

  • • Go to Settings > Apple ID
  • • Ensure they sign out of their Apple ID
  • • Check Find My iPhone is OFF
  • • Factory reset should complete without prompts

Android (Google FRP)

  • • Go to Settings > Accounts
  • • Remove all Google accounts
  • • Factory reset should complete without Google prompts
5

Inspect the Physical Phone at Meetup

When you meet the seller, complete these final verification steps:

  • Dial *#06# to confirm IMEI matches what you checked
  • Check for signs of tampering (scratches around SIM tray, screen bubbles)
  • Test all functions: screen, speakers, camera, buttons, ports
  • Insert your SIM card to verify it activates on your carrier

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check if a phone is stolen for free?

Use SafeOrStolen's free IMEI checker. Enter the 15-digit IMEI number and we'll verify it against carrier and theft databases and reported-stolen records. You get 2 free checks, no credit card required.

Where do I find the IMEI number on a phone?

Dial *#06# to display the IMEI. On iPhone: Settings > General > About. On Android: Settings > About Phone > Status. It's also on the original box or SIM tray.

What happens if I buy a stolen phone?

A stolen phone may be reported to police, who can confiscate it. The phone may also be blacklisted, preventing activation on any carrier network. You lose your money and the phone.

Can a stolen phone be unlocked?

No legitimate service can unlock a stolen phone. The IMEI remains blacklisted regardless of carrier unlock. iCloud/Google locks cannot be bypassed without original owner's credentials.

Is it illegal to buy a stolen phone?

Knowingly buying stolen property is illegal. Even accidental purchase can result in losing the phone to police. Always verify with SafeOrStolen before buying.
