What is Phone Verification?

An IMEI check is a verification process that uses a phone's unique 15-digit identifier to determine its status. Every mobile phone has a unique IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) number that can be checked against global databases to see if the device has been reported stolen, lost, blacklisted by carriers, or has outstanding finance. Running an IMEI check before buying a used phone protects you from purchasing a device that could be remotely disabled, confiscated, or unusable.

Why Check Before Buying?

In 2024, over 2.4 million smartphones were reported stolen in the US alone. Many of these end up on secondhand marketplaces. Buying a stolen phone means: the original owner can remotely lock or wipe it, carriers will blacklist it making it unusable, law enforcement can confiscate it, and you could face criminal possession charges. A quick IMEI check before purchase prevents all these problems.
