What is Phone Verification?

An IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) checker is a tool that verifies whether a mobile phone has been reported stolen, lost, or blacklisted by carriers. Every smartphone has a unique 15-digit IMEI number that can be used to track its status across global databases. SafeOrStolen's IMEI checker instantly searches multiple databases to determine if a phone is safe to buy or if it's been reported as stolen or lost. This protects buyers from purchasing phones that could be remotely locked, reported to authorities, or unusable on cellular networks.

Our IMEI checker works by querying multiple international databases simultaneously, including carrier and manufacturer blacklists, community theft registries, and user-reported theft databases. Within 3 seconds, you'll receive a comprehensive report showing whether the phone is clean, stolen, blacklisted, or has outstanding finance. We also check if the phone has been reported lost, if it's locked to a specific carrier, and if there are any insurance claims filed against it.

Why Check Before Buying?

Buying a stolen phone can lead to serious consequences. The phone can be remotely locked or wiped by the original owner, blacklisted by carriers making it unusable, confiscated by law enforcement as stolen property, and you could face criminal charges for possession of stolen goods. Additionally, you lose all your money with zero legal recourse. In 2024 alone, over 2.4 million smartphones were reported stolen in the United States, with many ending up on secondhand marketplaces like Facebook Marketplace, eBay, OfferUp, and Craigslist. Our free IMEI checker helps you avoid these risks.
