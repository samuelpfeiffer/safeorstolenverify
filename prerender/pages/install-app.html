Installation FAQs

Why isn't SafeOrStolen in the App Store?

SafeOrStolen is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that installs directly from your browser. This means instant updates, no app store approval delays, and works on ALL devices including iPhone, Android, tablets, and computers.

Is installing SafeOrStolen safe?

Yes! PWAs are sandboxed by your browser with the same security as regular websites. SafeOrStolen doesn't require any special permissions and can't access your personal data without consent.

Will SafeOrStolen work offline?

Yes. Once installed, SafeOrStolen caches recent verifications so you can access them without internet—perfect for marketplace meetups in areas with poor signal.

How much storage does SafeOrStolen use?

Less than 5MB. SafeOrStolen is lightweight and won't slow down your device or consume significant storage space.

Can I uninstall SafeOrStolen?

Yes, just like any app. iPhone: Long-press the icon → Remove App. Android: Long-press → Uninstall. Desktop: Right-click → Uninstall.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required for your first 2 free checks. Create a free account to save your verification history and access unlimited checks for $10/month.
