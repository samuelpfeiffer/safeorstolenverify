What is iPhone Verification?

Checking if an iPhone is stolen means verifying the device hasn't been reported to Apple, carriers, or police as lost or stolen property. Every iPhone has two unique identifiers: an IMEI number (15 digits) and a serial number (alphanumeric). When an iPhone is reported stolen, it gets added to the carrier and theft databases global blacklist, Apple's activation lock database, and carrier ban lists. A stolen iPhone can be remotely locked, tracked, or completely bricked by the original owner. SafeOrStolen's iPhone checker searches all these databases simultaneously to give you a definitive answer: Is this iPhone safe to buy, or is it stolen?

Why Check Before Buying?

Buying a stolen iPhone is a guaranteed disaster. Here's what happens: The original owner can remotely lock or erase the iPhone at any time using Find My iPhone. Apple will refuse all service, including repairs and battery replacements. Carriers will blacklist the IMEI, making it unusable on any US network. Police can seize the iPhone as evidence with no compensation to you. You could face criminal charges for possession of stolen property. In 2024, over 800,000 iPhones were reported stolen in the United States alone. Many end up on Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, OfferUp, and eBay. Scammers specifically target buyers who don't verify before purchasing. A 3-second check could save you hundreds or thousands of dollars.
