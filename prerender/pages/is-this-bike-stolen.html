Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the bicycle serial number?

Usually located under the bottom bracket (where the pedal cranks meet), on the head tube, or rear dropout.

What if the serial number is filed off?

A removed serial number is a major red flag indicating the bike may be stolen. Do not purchase.

Does this work for e-bikes?

Yes, we can verify electric bikes, road bikes, mountain bikes, and all bicycle types.

How do I report a stolen bike?

Report to local police first, then register it on stolen bike databases. We can help with verification after recovery.
