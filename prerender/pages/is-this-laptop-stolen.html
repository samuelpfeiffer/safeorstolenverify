Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find the laptop serial number?

Check the bottom of the laptop, in System Settings/About, or on the original box. For MacBooks: Apple Menu > About This Mac.

What types of laptops can I check?

We can verify Apple MacBooks, Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, and most major laptop brands.

Should I meet the seller in person?

Yes, always meet in a public place and verify the serial number matches what you checked before paying.

What if the seller won't provide the serial?

This is a major red flag. Legitimate sellers have nothing to hide. Walk away from the deal.
