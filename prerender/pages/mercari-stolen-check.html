Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify Mercari electronics?

Message the seller requesting the IMEI (for phones) or serial number (for electronics). Enter it into SafeOrStolen's free verification tool before purchasing.

Does Mercari check for stolen items?

Mercari has some protections, but they don't verify serial numbers against stolen databases. You need to verify yourself using SafeOrStolen.

What if I already bought a stolen item on Mercari?

Contact Mercari support immediately. Do not confront the seller. File a police report. You may be able to get a refund through Mercari's protection program.

Can I return stolen items on Mercari?

Mercari's return policy may apply, but if the item is stolen, it may be confiscated by authorities. Always verify before buying to avoid this situation.
