About SafeOrStolen

SafeOrStolen is a technology company dedicated to protecting buyers and sellers in the secondhand market from stolen goods. Our platform provides instant verification of phones (IMEI), vehicles (VIN), and any item with a serial number against a database of 50+ million stolen items aggregated from law enforcement agencies, insurance companies, and carrier blacklists.

Founded in 2019, SafeOrStolen has grown to become a trusted verification service used by individual buyers, marketplaces, pawn shops, and insurance companies. Our mobile apps for iOS and Android feature barcode scanning for instant 3-second verification, making due diligence accessible to everyone.

Our mission is simple: ensure that no one unknowingly buys or sells stolen property. The secondhand market represents over $150 billion annually in the US alone, and an estimated 5-15% of items sold in unstructured marketplaces like Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and OfferUp have questionable origins.
