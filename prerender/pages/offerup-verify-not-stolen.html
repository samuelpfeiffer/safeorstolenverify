TruYou Doesn't Verify Items - Only Sellers

Many buyers mistakenly believe OfferUp's TruYou badge means items are verified safe. This is incorrect.

  • TruYou only verifies the seller's identity (phone, email, Facebook)
  • TruYou does NOT check if items are stolen
  • A verified seller can still unknowingly (or knowingly) sell stolen goods
  • Solution: Use SafeOrStolen to verify the actual item before purchase

OfferUp Verification FAQs

How do I check if an OfferUp item is stolen?

Ask the seller for the item's IMEI (phones), VIN (vehicles), or serial number (electronics). Enter it into SafeOrStolen's free verification tool to check against stolen property databases.

Does OfferUp verify items aren't stolen?

No. OfferUp does not verify whether items are stolen. Their TruYou verification only confirms seller identity, not item legitimacy. You must verify items yourself using SafeOrStolen.

What happens if I buy stolen goods on OfferUp?

If police recover the item, it will be confiscated and returned to the original owner. You'll lose both the item and your money. You may also face criminal charges for receiving stolen property.

Is OfferUp safer than Craigslist?

OfferUp's TruYou verification and in-app messaging provide some accountability, but stolen items are still common. Always verify items with SafeOrStolen regardless of the platform.

Can I use SafeOrStolen at an OfferUp meetup?

Yes! Download the SafeOrStolen app to verify items on-the-spot. Scan the barcode or enter the identifier to get instant results before paying.

What items should I verify on OfferUp?

Verify all high-value items: phones (IMEI), vehicles (VIN), laptops, tablets, gaming consoles, bikes, power tools, and any electronics with serial numbers.
