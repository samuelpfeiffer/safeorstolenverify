Frequently Asked Questions

Does Poshmark authenticate items?

Poshmark Authenticate is available for some luxury items, but it doesn't check against stolen property databases. You should verify separately.

How do I verify Rolex on Poshmark?

Request the Rolex serial number (between the lugs or on warranty card). Enter it into SafeOrStolen to check The Watch Register and stolen databases.

What if a Poshmark item is stolen?

Don't complete the purchase. Report the listing to Poshmark and consider notifying police. The original owner may be searching for their item.

Can jewelry be verified?

Yes, if it has identifying marks. GIA-certified diamonds have laser inscriptions. Luxury watches have serial numbers. Check any available identifiers.
