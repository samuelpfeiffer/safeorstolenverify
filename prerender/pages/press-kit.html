About SafeOrStolen

SafeOrStolen is a SaaS stolen-item verification platform that allows users to instantly check if secondhand items are reported as lost, stolen, or safe to purchase. The platform supports IMEI, VIN, and serial number verification across all 50 US states with global database coverage.

Founded in 2024 and backed by FasterCapital, SafeOrStolen is the only solution in the market with a dedicated mobile app featuring barcode scanning technology. The platform serves individual buyers and sellers on marketplaces like Facebook Marketplace, eBay, and OfferUp, as well as businesses including pawn shops, auto dealers, and auction houses.

Mission: To protect buyers, sellers, and businesses from financial loss, legal trouble, and involvement with stolen goods - ultimately reducing stolen goods crime globally.
