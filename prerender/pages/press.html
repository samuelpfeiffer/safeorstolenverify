About SafeOrStolen

SafeOrStolen is a technology company on a mission to eliminate the trade of stolen goods in secondhand markets. We provide free and low-cost verification tools that allow anyone to check if a phone, vehicle, or other item has been reported stolen before they buy it.

Our platform searches over 100 databases including global device registries, federal law enforcement records, carrier and theft databases, and insurance claims databases to provide comprehensive verification in under 3 seconds.

Founded to protect buyers on Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist, and other secondhand platforms, Most stolen-item tools cover one category. SafeOrStolen covers phones, vehicles, license plates, electronics, power tools, firearms, and bicycles in one place.
