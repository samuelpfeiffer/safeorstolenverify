Pricing FAQ

Do I get any free checks?▼
Yes! Every new user gets 2 free verifications — no credit card required. After that, pay $0.99 per check or go unlimited for $10/month.
Can I cancel the unlimited plan anytime?▼
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard with one click. Your plan continues until the end of the billing period — no fees, no hassle.
What counts as one verification?▼
Each IMEI, VIN, serial number, plate, or firearm serial submitted counts as one check. Viewing the same result again is free.
How accurate are the results?▼
We check 100+ sources including carrier and theft databases, vehicle theft and title databases, and all 50 US state databases. ~99.2% accuracy.
Do you offer a money-back guarantee?▼
Yes — 30-day full refund on the unlimited plan, no questions asked.
Do I need an account for pay-per-check?▼
No. Pay $0.99, enter your item's identifier, and get instant results. No account, no sign-up, no recurring charges.
How do seller verification badges work?▼
Seller badges are included free with the $10/month unlimited plan. Verify your item, generate a badge, and paste it into your marketplace listing. Buyers click it to see the verified result.
Which plan should I choose?▼
If you're checking one item before buying, use a free check or pay $0.99. If you buy and sell secondhand items regularly (3+ times per month), the $10/month unlimited plan saves you money.
