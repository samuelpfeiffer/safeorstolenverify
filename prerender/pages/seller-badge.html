Frequently Asked Questions

Is the seller badge free?

Your first 2 verifications are free, which includes badge generation. After that, $0.99/check or $10/month unlimited.

How do I add the badge to my listing?

After generating your badge, you'll get a shareable link (paste it in your listing description) and a badge image (download and add to your listing photos). Both link to your full verification report on SafeOrStolen.

Can buyers verify that my badge is real?

Yes. Every badge has a unique verification ID. When buyers click the badge or visit the link, they see the full verification report with timestamp, databases checked, and result — confirming the badge is authentic.
