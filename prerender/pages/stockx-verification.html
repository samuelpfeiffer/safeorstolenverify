Frequently Asked Questions

Does StockX check for stolen items?

StockX authenticates products for legitimacy but doesn't verify serial numbers against stolen property databases. For electronics, use SafeOrStolen.

Can I verify gaming consoles?

Yes! PS5, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch consoles have serial numbers. Verify before purchasing to ensure they're not stolen or banned from online services.

What resale platforms should I verify on?

Any platform: StockX, GOAT, eBay, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist. If buying electronics or items with serial numbers, always verify.

How do I find serial numbers on gaming consoles?

Check the back/bottom of the console, look in system settings, or ask the seller to provide photos of the serial number sticker.
