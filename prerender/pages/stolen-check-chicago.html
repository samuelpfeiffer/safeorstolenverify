Frequently Asked Questions - Chicago

How do I check if a car in Chicago is stolen?

Use SafeOrStolen's free VIN checker. Chicago has over 20,000 vehicle thefts annually. Kia and Hyundai vehicles are especially targeted.

Where can I safely meet sellers in Chicago?

CPD offers safe exchange zones at district stations. Popular options include the 1st District (Central), 19th District (Town Hall), and 25th District (Grand Central).

Is catalytic converter theft affecting Chicago?

Yes. Chicago is a hotspot for catalytic converter theft. If buying a used car, verify it hasn't been reported stolen and check for converter damage.

What neighborhoods have the highest theft rates?

Englewood, Austin, North Lawndale, and South Shore have higher theft rates. However, stolen items are sold throughout the city, so always verify regardless of location.
