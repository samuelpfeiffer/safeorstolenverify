Why Verify Before Buying in Dallas?

The Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex has one of the highest vehicle theft rates in the nation. With a massive secondhand market across Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, and Craigslist, stolen goods frequently end up for sale to unsuspecting buyers.

The "Kia Boys" trend has made older Kia and Hyundai vehicles especially vulnerable. If you're buying any used vehicle in DFW, a quick VIN check can save you from purchasing a stolen car and facing legal consequences.

Frequently Asked Questions - Dallas

How bad is vehicle theft in Dallas-Fort Worth?

Dallas-Fort Worth is consistently one of the top 5 metros in the US for vehicle theft with over 30,000 thefts annually across the metroplex. Kia and Hyundai vehicles are especially targeted.

Where can I safely meet sellers in Dallas?

Dallas PD offers safe exchange zones at police stations. The Jack Evans Police Headquarters and various substations have designated areas. Many fire stations also welcome safe exchanges.

What items are commonly stolen in Dallas?

Vehicles, catalytic converters, construction equipment, and electronics top the list. The thriving resale market makes verification essential for any used purchase.

Is the Kia/Hyundai theft trend affecting Dallas?

Yes, significantly. Dallas has seen a surge in Kia and Hyundai thefts due to the 'Kia Boys' trend. Always verify VIN on any 2011-2021 Kia or Hyundai before purchasing.
