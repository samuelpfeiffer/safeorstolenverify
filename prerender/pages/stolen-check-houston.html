Why Verify Before Buying in Houston?

Houston's sprawling metro area and thriving economy create one of the largest secondhand markets in Texas. Unfortunately, this also means stolen goods frequently appear for sale on Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, and Craigslist.

The city's massive construction industry makes power tools especially vulnerable. Catalytic converter theft has also surged, making it crucial to verify any used vehicle purchase. SafeOrStolen's free verification takes just 3 seconds and checks against national databases.

HPD Safe Exchange Zones

The Houston Police Department offers safe exchange zones at police substations throughout the city. Popular locations include the Central Station downtown, Clear Lake Station, and Memorial Station. Even at safe zones, always verify items before completing the transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions - Houston

How do I check if a car in Houston is stolen?

Use SafeOrStolen's free VIN checker. Houston is one of the top 10 US cities for vehicle theft with over 35,000 annually. Full-size trucks are especially targeted.

Where are Houston's safe exchange zones?

HPD offers safe exchange locations at many police substations. Popular options include the Central Station downtown and substations in Clear Lake and Memorial.

What vehicles are most stolen in Houston?

Full-size pickup trucks (F-150, Silverado, Ram), Kia/Hyundai sedans, and Nissan Altimas are the most stolen vehicles in Houston.

Is tool theft common in Houston?

Yes. Houston's construction industry makes it a target for power tool theft. Always verify serial numbers when buying tools from individuals.
