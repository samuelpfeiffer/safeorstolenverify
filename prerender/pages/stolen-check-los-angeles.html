Los Angeles Verification FAQs

How do I check if a used car in Los Angeles is stolen?

Use SafeOrStolen's free VIN checker. Enter the 17-digit VIN to verify against LAPD and California NHTSA vehicle records. California has the highest vehicle theft rate in the nation.

Where should I meet sellers in LA to buy used items?

LAPD offers Safe Exchange Zones at police stations throughout LA. Popular locations include Hollywood Division, Pacific Division, and Van Nuys. Always verify items before meeting.

Is phone theft common in Los Angeles?

Yes. LA ranks among the top US cities for smartphone theft, especially in tourist areas like Hollywood and Venice Beach. Always check IMEI before buying a used phone.

What's the stolen vehicle rate in Los Angeles?

LA has over 58,000 vehicle thefts annually, the highest in California. Popular targets include Honda Civics, Toyota Camrys, and Kia/Hyundai vehicles.

Can I use SafeOrStolen at swap meets in LA?

Yes! Download the SafeOrStolen app to verify items at Pomona Swap Meet, Roadium, or any LA swap meet. The app works offline with barcode scanning.
