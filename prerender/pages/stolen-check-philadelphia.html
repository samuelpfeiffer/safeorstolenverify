Why Verify Before Buying in Philadelphia?

Philadelphia has experienced a dramatic surge in vehicle theft, largely driven by the "Kia Boys" trend that went viral on TikTok. This has made Kia and Hyundai vehicles from 2011-2021 especially vulnerable, as they lack engine immobilizers.

The city's active secondhand market means stolen goods frequently appear for sale. SafeOrStolen's free verification helps you avoid purchasing stolen vehicles or devices. Our check takes just 3 seconds and searches against national databases.

PPD Safe Exchange Zones

The Philadelphia Police Department offers safe exchange zones at district headquarters throughout the city. The 6th District in Center City and 35th District in Northeast Philly are popular options. Always verify items with SafeOrStolen before completing any transaction, even at safe zones.
