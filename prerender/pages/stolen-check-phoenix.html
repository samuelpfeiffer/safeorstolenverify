Phoenix Verification FAQs

How bad is vehicle theft in Phoenix?

Phoenix consistently ranks in the top 10 US cities for vehicle theft per capita with over 14,000 thefts annually. Always verify VIN before purchasing.

Where can I safely meet sellers in Phoenix?

Phoenix PD and surrounding cities offer safe exchange zones. The Phoenix Police HQ at 620 W Washington and various precincts offer monitored meetup locations.

Is motorcycle theft common in Phoenix?

Yes. Arizona's year-round riding weather makes it a hotspot for motorcycle theft. Always verify the VIN of any motorcycle purchase.

What about stolen items from Mexico?

Phoenix's proximity to the border means some stolen vehicles are trafficked. Always verify VIN to ensure the vehicle isn't stolen or cloned.
