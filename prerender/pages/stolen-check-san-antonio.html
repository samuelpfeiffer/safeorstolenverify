Frequently Asked Questions - San Antonio

How common is vehicle theft in San Antonio?

San Antonio reports over 11,000 vehicle thefts annually, ranking it among Texas's highest for auto theft. Trucks and SUVs are particularly targeted due to their resale value.

Where can I safely meet sellers in San Antonio?

SAPD offers safe exchange zones at police substations throughout the city. The Public Safety Headquarters at 315 S Santa Rosa Ave is a popular option. Many H-E-B parking lots also have security cameras.

What items are commonly stolen in San Antonio?

Beyond vehicles, catalytic converters, power tools, bicycles, and electronics are frequently stolen. Always verify serial numbers before purchasing used items.

Is phone theft a problem in San Antonio?

Yes, especially during festivals like Fiesta. Always verify IMEI numbers before buying used phones to avoid purchasing stolen devices.
