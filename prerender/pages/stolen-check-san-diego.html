Why Verify Before Buying in San Diego?

San Diego's unique position along the U.S.-Mexico border creates additional risks for secondhand buyers. Stolen vehicles can be transported across the border within hours, making recovery nearly impossible. This also means that "cloned" VINs from legitimate vehicles are sometimes used on stolen cars.

SafeOrStolen checks against multiple databases including vehicle theft and title databases, police reports, and insurance claims to help identify potentially stolen items. Our free verification takes just 3 seconds and could save you from purchasing a stolen vehicle or device.

SDPD Safe Exchange Zones

The San Diego Police Department offers safe exchange zones at police stations throughout the city. Popular locations include the Downtown Central Division, Northern Division in Scripps Ranch, and the Mid-City Division. Always verify items with SafeOrStolen before completing any transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions - San Diego

Why is vehicle theft higher in San Diego?

San Diego's proximity to the Mexican border makes it a prime location for vehicle trafficking. Stolen cars can be across the border within hours, making recovery difficult.

Where can I safely meet sellers in San Diego?

SDPD offers safe exchange zones at police stations including the Downtown Central Division and Northern Division. Many fire stations also welcome safe exchanges.

What vehicles are most targeted in San Diego?

Full-size trucks (especially F-150s and Silverados), Toyota Camrys, and Honda Civics are frequently stolen. Newer SUVs and crossovers are also targeted.

Is motorcycle theft common in San Diego?

Yes. San Diego's year-round riding weather makes motorcycles prime targets. Always verify VIN before purchasing any motorcycle in the San Diego area.
