Why Verify Before Buying in San Jose?

Silicon Valley's concentration of tech workers makes San Jose a prime target for electronics theft. Car break-ins targeting laptops, phones, and tablets are extremely common, especially in downtown parking lots and near tech company offices. These stolen devices often end up for sale on Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, and Craigslist.

SafeOrStolen helps you verify if a device has been reported stolen before you buy. For Apple devices, we recommend also checking the Find My status and Activation Lock. Our free verification takes just 3 seconds.

SJPD Safe Exchange Zones

The San Jose Police Department offers safe exchange zones at substations throughout the city. For electronics purchases, consider meeting at the Apple Store lobby (Valley Fair or Santana Row) during business hours, as staff can help verify device legitimacy.

Frequently Asked Questions - San Jose

What electronics are most commonly stolen in San Jose?

MacBooks, iPhones, iPads, and gaming laptops are the most frequently stolen electronics in Silicon Valley. Car break-ins targeting tech workers are common, so always verify serial numbers.

Where can I safely meet sellers in San Jose?

SJPD offers safe exchange zones at police substations. The Downtown Division and Evergreen Division are popular choices. Apple Store lobbies are also recommended for electronics.

Is vehicle theft common in San Jose?

Yes. San Jose sees over 6,000 vehicle thefts annually. Catalytic converter theft is also rampant, particularly targeting Toyota Prius and Honda vehicles.

How do I verify a used laptop before buying?

Use SafeOrStolen to check the serial number. For MacBooks, also verify the Find My status is disabled and the device isn't Activation Locked.
