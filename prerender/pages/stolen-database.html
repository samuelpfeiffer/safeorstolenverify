About Our Stolen Items Database

The SafeOrStolen database is the largest publicly searchable collection of reported stolen items. Our database includes phones, laptops, vehicles, bicycles, firearms, jewelry, and more. Every item is verified through our network of law enforcement partnerships and user reports.

How to Use the Database

  • Search by identifier: Enter a serial number, IMEI, or VIN to find exact matches
  • Browse by category: Filter by item type to find specific categories
  • Search by location: Narrow results to your city or state
  • Report a stolen item: Add your stolen property to help others avoid purchasing it

Help Recover Stolen Property

If you recognize an item in our database or have information about a theft, please contact local law enforcement. Every search helps reduce the market for stolen goods and increases the chances of recovery for theft victims.
