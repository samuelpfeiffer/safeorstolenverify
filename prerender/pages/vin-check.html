What is Car Verification?

A VIN check is a verification that uses a vehicle's 17-character Vehicle Identification Number to determine if it's been reported stolen, salvaged, or has title issues. Every vehicle manufactured since 1981 has a unique VIN that's registered in national databases. Running a VIN check before buying a used vehicle protects you from purchasing stolen property, salvage frauds, or vehicles with hidden liens.

Why Check Before Buying?

Over 800,000 vehicles are stolen in the US each year. Many are sold to unsuspecting buyers who lose their money and the vehicle when police recover it. A stolen vehicle will be seized by law enforcement regardless of whether you paid for it, and you have no legal recourse to recover your money from the seller. A 30-second VIN check prevents this nightmare scenario.
