FastBound

Who it's for: Federal Firearms Licensees (FFLs) — gun dealers, pawn shops, and manufacturers with an ATF license.

What it does: SafeOrStolen checks stolen firearm reports from all 50 states (~32,000 victim-reported records) and validates serial number integrity. NCIC and ATF eTrace are restricted to law enforcement, so for a definitive check ask your local police non-emergency line to run the serial.

Limitation: Consumers cannot use FastBound. You must be a licensed FFL.

SafeOrStolen

Who it's for: Anyone — consumers buying used firearms from private sellers, pawn shops, or online marketplaces.

What it does: Verifies firearm serial numbers against databases to check if a gun has been reported stolen. Also checks phones, cars, bikes, and more.

Advantage: No license required. Free searches available. Mobile app for in-person verification.

When to Use Each Tool

Use FastBound If:

  • You're a licensed firearms dealer (FFL)
  • You need ATF compliance software
  • You manage dealer inventory and 4473 forms
  • You need NCIC access for business operations

Use SafeOrStolen If:

  • You're a consumer buying a used firearm
  • You want to verify before a private sale
  • You also need to check phones, cars, or bikes
  • You want a mobile app for in-person meetups

The Consumer Gap in Firearm Verification

The NCIC stolen gun database is only accessible to law enforcement and licensed dealers. That means if you're buying a used gun from a private seller, you have no way to verify it's not stolen — unless you use SafeOrStolen.

We aggregate data from user-submitted reports, 50-State Firearm Reports, and other sources to give consumers a way to check firearm serial numbers before making a purchase.

Remember: If you unknowingly buy a stolen firearm, it can be confiscated by police — and you lose your money.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use FastBound as a regular consumer?

No. FastBound requires an active Federal Firearms License (FFL). It's designed for gun dealers, not individual consumers. SafeOrStolen is available to everyone.

Does SafeOrStolen access NCIC?

SafeOrStolen uses multiple databases including user-submitted stolen reports and public registries. While we can't access NCIC directly (that's restricted to law enforcement), we provide valuable stolen status signals for consumer due diligence.

Is 50-State Firearm Reports better than SafeOrStolen?

50-State Firearm Reports only checks firearms. SafeOrStolen checks firearms AND phones, cars, bikes, laptops, and more. Plus, we have a mobile app for verification at meetups. See our 50-State Firearm Reports comparison.
