What information can I find with a DC plate lookup?

A DC license plate lookup reveals: vehicle make, model, and year; registration status; theft reports from Metropolitan Police; salvage title history; and whether the vehicle has been in major accidents. This helps you verify any used vehicle before making a purchase in the DC area.

Why does DC have "Taxation Without Representation" on plates?

Washington DC residents pay federal taxes but have no voting representation in Congress. The "Taxation Without Representation" slogan on DC license plates (since 2000) is a protest statement. This doesn't affect our plate lookup - all DC plates with this slogan are fully searchable in our system.
