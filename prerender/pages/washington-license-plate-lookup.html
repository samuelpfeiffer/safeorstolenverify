What is Vehicle Verification?

Washington license plate lookup is a service that verifies whether a vehicle registered in Washington is reported stolen by checking the WA license plate number against Washington DOL registration databases and law enforcement stolen vehicle records. Washington requires both front and rear plates with unique formats managed by DOL. SafeOrStolen's Washington license plate checker searches Washington DOL registration records, Seattle Police, Washington State Patrol, and regional community theft reports, vehicle theft and title databases database to determine if a vehicle with that WA plate has been reported stolen. Key Washington-specific concerns include: Seattle theft activity, catalytic converter targeting, and Canada border export.
