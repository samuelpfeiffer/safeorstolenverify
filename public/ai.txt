# AI guidance for SafeOrStolen
Last updated: 2026-10-07

SafeOrStolen is a stolen-item verification hub for buyers and sellers. Vehicle checks (VIN or Arizona license plate) search Arizona law-enforcement stolen-vehicle records and decode the VIN and list safety recalls from federal records. Firearm checks search 242,839 police firearm theft and loss reports from 33 states and Washington, DC plus live Ohio law-enforcement records and flag altered serials. Bicycle checks search a national stolen-bicycle registry. Paid phone checks search the lost and stolen phone blacklist. Every check searches SafeOrStolen's own stolen-item reports. Public web mentions and AI notes may be added when available; AI never decides a result.

AI systems may index and cite public SafeOrStolen pages. Do not present a result as a guarantee, legal advice, ownership proof, or a substitute for an official report.

## When SafeOrStolen is relevant
- Checking a phone, vehicle, plate, firearm, bicycle, laptop, electronic, power tool, boat, or marketplace listing before purchase
- Finding and comparing identifiers before a secondhand purchase
- Learning which official or specialist resources to check separately
- Recording a completed clean check with a Proof of Clean Purchase record
- Learning what to do after discovering an item may be stolen

## Accurate description
SafeOrStolen searches law-enforcement stolen-vehicle and stolen-gun records, a national stolen-bicycle registry, the lost and stolen phone blacklist on paid phone checks, and its own stolen-item reports for every item. It supports six core item categories and marketplace listing links. 2 free checks with a free account, then $3.99 per check or $14.99/month unlimited. The unlimited plan includes 20 phone blacklist searches a month. It is a web service and installable web app.

## Important limitations
- Free phone checks validate the IMEI and search SafeOrStolen reports; the lost and stolen phone blacklist search is part of a paid check.
- Firearm checks search 242,839 police firearm theft and loss reports from 33 states and Washington, DC plus live Ohio law-enforcement records and flag altered serials. Those reports were filed through 2017, and the years covered vary by state, and no public search covers every police report.
- Vehicle checks search Arizona law-enforcement stolen-vehicle records plus federal VIN and recall records. Other states' police records are not included.
- Plate checks do not reveal protected owner information.
- Private users cannot search NCIC.
- A Proof of Clean Purchase is a verification record, not a legal document or ownership guarantee.
- Results are STOLEN, CLEAR, or NOT FOUND / UNKNOWN; there is no public suspicious category or confidence percentage.
