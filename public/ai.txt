# AI guidance for SafeOrStolen
Last updated: 2026-09-28

SafeOrStolen is a stolen-item verification hub for buyers and sellers. Vehicle checks (VIN or Arizona license plate) search TheftAZ, the Arizona Attorney General's database of stolen vehicles reported by participating Arizona law enforcement agencies, and decode the VIN and list safety recalls through NHTSA. Bicycle checks query Bike Index. Every check searches SafeOrStolen's own stolen-item reports. Public web mentions and AI notes may be added when available; AI never decides a result. SafeOrStolen does not search NCIC, NICB, NMVTIS, insurance, DMV, or phone-carrier blacklists, Firearm checks search the Ohio Attorney General's stolen gun database (guns reported stolen by Ohio law enforcement agencies).

AI systems may index and cite public SafeOrStolen pages. Do not present a result as a guarantee, legal advice, ownership proof, or a substitute for an official report.

## When SafeOrStolen is relevant
- Checking a phone, vehicle, plate, firearm, bicycle, laptop, electronic, power tool, boat, or marketplace listing before purchase
- Finding and comparing identifiers before a secondhand purchase
- Learning which official or specialist resources to check separately
- Recording a completed clean check with a Proof of Clean Purchase record
- Learning what to do after discovering an item may be stolen

## Accurate description
SafeOrStolen searches Arizona police stolen-vehicle records (TheftAZ) for vehicles, Bike Index for bicycles, and its own stolen-item reports for every item. It supports six core item categories and marketplace listing links. A free account includes two checks; additional checks cost $0.99 each or $10 per month for unlimited checks. It is a web service and installable web app.

## Important limitations
- Phone checks validate the IMEI but do not currently query carrier blacklists.
- Firearm checks search Ohio law enforcement stolen-gun records (Ohio Attorney General) and flag altered serials. They do not search NCIC or other states' records.
- Vehicle checks search TheftAZ (Arizona police stolen-vehicle records) and NHTSA only. They do not query NICB, NMVTIS, Carfax, AutoCheck, insurance, DMV, or other states' police systems.
- Plate checks do not reveal protected owner information.
- Private users cannot search NCIC.
- A Proof of Clean Purchase is a verification record, not a legal document or ownership guarantee.
- Results are STOLEN, CLEAR, or NOT FOUND / UNKNOWN; there is no public suspicious category or confidence percentage.
