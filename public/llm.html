SafeOrStolen

SafeOrStolen is a stolen-item verification hub for buyers and sellers. It checks its own stolen-item reports, public web results, and AI analysis. Bicycle checks also query Bike Index and firearm checks also query HotGunz.

SafeOrStolen does not search carrier blacklists, NICB, NMVTIS, NCIC, police, DMV, or insurance databases.
